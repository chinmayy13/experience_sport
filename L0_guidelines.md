# 🧭 L0 Guidelines: Human vs. AD1 SxS Evaluation

**The one question you're answering:** Is this human deliverable good enough, and hard enough for AI, that it's worth buying for AI training?

## ⚡ TL;DR

1. Open the Autoflation Link. It shows the brief, inputs, RD, and AD1 side by side. Keep it open.
2. Answer the gate: **Is this task appropriate to rate?**
   a. **Yes** → continue to the SxS.
   b. **No** → the submission is spam (fake, stolen, unrelated, inappropriate or clearly low effort), or there's nothing to rate (the RD is missing, or the brief or inputs are unusable). Anything else can be rated or sent back with Needs Fixes. Explain why → Quality Measurement → submit. The task is wiped and sent to L-1 clean.
   c. **Broken Autoflation Link** → describe what's broken → submit. Everything else is skipped.
   d. **Needs Fixes** → the task has potential but needs fixes or files. List them → Quality Measurement → submit. It goes back to the same contributor with their work kept.
3. Score 5 dimensions (1–7) and write one justification paragraph (150+ characters).
4. Decide: **Go · No Go · Need More Information / Fixes**, and fill in the box that opens.
5. On Go, review the Updated Brief. It's prefilled with the original brief.
6. Complete the Quality Measurement: a 1–5 score and feedback for the contributor. Required on every path except Broken Autoflation Link.

🔑 **1 = human wins · 4 = tie · 7 = AD1 wins.** A tie is a No Go. If all five scores are 4 or higher, the tool pre-selects No Go.

## 1. 🎯 Why This Task Exists

We're deciding which human-made deliverables (RD) to move forward for AI training. An RD is only valuable if it shows something AI can't do yet. To test that, an AI model gets the same brief and inputs as the human and produces its own version, AD1. You compare the two.

| Label | What it is |
|---|---|
| **RD (Human Deliverable)** | The files submitted by the contributor (a human professional) for the brief |
| **AD1 (Alternative Deliverable 1)** | The artifact the AI model generated from the same brief and input files |

### Every possible outcome:

| If… | You choose… | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 RD clearly beats AD1 | **Go** | RD is moved forward. You review the Updated Brief. |
| 🔴 AD1 matches or beats RD | **No Go** | The response is cleared and the task goes to L-1. |
| 🟡 A problem only shows up during the SxS | **Need More Information / Fixes** | The task goes back to the same contributor with their work kept, plus your notes. |
| ⛔ The submission is spam, or there's nothing to rate | **No** at the gate | The task is wiped and sent to L-1 clean. |
| 🛠 The Autoflation Link is broken | **Broken Autoflation Link** at the gate | The task is sent to be fixed. The rest is skipped. |
| 🔧 You can see right away that the task needs fixes or files | **Needs Fixes** at the gate | The task goes back to the same contributor with their work kept. |

## 4. 🚦 Step 2: Is This Task Appropriate to Rate?

Pick one of four options. Every option except Yes opens a required text box.

| Choose | When | Box to fill in | What happens next |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yes** | Everything loads and the task can be rated fairly | — | Continue to the SxS |
| **No** | The submission is spam (fake, stolen, unrelated, inappropriate or clearly low effort), or there's nothing to rate (the RD is missing, or the brief or inputs are unusable). Anything else can be rated or sent back with Needs Fixes. | "Why is this task not appropriate to rate?" (30+ characters) + Quality Measurement | The task is wiped and sent to L-1 clean |
| **Broken Autoflation Link** | The link doesn't load, is empty, or doesn't show RD or AD1 | "What is broken in the Autoflation Link?" (20+ characters). No Quality Measurement. | The task is sent to be fixed. The rest is skipped. |
| **Needs Fixes** | The task has potential, but the contributor must fix something or add files first | "What needs to be fixed?" (30+ characters, shown to the contributor) + Quality Measurement | It goes back to the same contributor with their work kept |

### ⚠️ No vs. Needs Fixes

**No** wipes the task and sends it to L-1 clean. If the task has potential and you want to send it back (SBQ) for improvements or more files, don't choose No. Choose **Needs Fixes** instead.

### ℹ️ Needs Fixes vs. Need More Information / Fixes

Both send the task back to the same contributor with their work kept. The difference is when you spot the problem:

- **Needs Fixes** (at the gate): you can tell at first sight, before any scoring.
- **Need More Information / Fixes** (at the decision): the problem only shows up once you're into the SxS, for example when the brief turns out too unclear to compare fairly. Answer Yes at the gate, do the SxS, then choose it as your decision.

## 5. 📏 Step 3: SxS Comparison

### The scale

Each dimension is a slider from 1 to 7. The left end is labeled "Human Better," the middle "Comparable," and the right end "AD1 Better."

| Score | Meaning | Leans toward |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | RD (human) is significantly better | 🟢 Human |
| 2–3 | RD (human) is somewhat better | 🟢 Human |
| 4 | Comparable quality | ⚪ Neither (tie) |
| 5–6 | AD1 is somewhat better | 🔴 AD1 |
| 7 | AD1 is significantly better | 🔴 AD1 |

### The 5 dimensions

Score every dimension on its own, and score all five before you decide.

| # | Dimension | Ask yourself… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Realism | Does it look, sound, or feel natural and true to life, like a human professional made it? |
| 2 | Design and UI/UX Quality | Is it well made? Think composition, visual or audio appeal, and how easy it is to engage with. |
| 3 | Professionalism | Is it polished, free of errors and artifacts, and ready for client delivery or production use? |
| 4 | Coherence and Continuity | Is it internally consistent and logically structured, with no jarring transitions or contradictions? |
| 5 | Multi-Asset Consistency | Do all components or assets share one style and quality level? (See the box below.) |

ℹ️ **What counts as an "asset"?** Any distinct visual component, not a delivered file.

- Within one image: the illustrated mark, any wordmark or text, a tagline, a badge, and a distinct background treatment are each a separate asset.
- Worked example: a logo with a stylized boat hull, the boat's name as a wordmark, and a tagline = 3 assets, even though it's one image. Do they read as one set?
- Multiple image files: each delivered image (a PNG and a JPG of the same mark, or the logo on several backgrounds) also counts. Non-image files (a rationale doc) don't.
- True single asset: a solo mark with no text, no tagline, and no secondary treatment. This is the only case you can't score. Score it 4 and say so in your justification.
- Gray area: if components touch or are barely distinct, you can treat them as one asset. Say which reading you used.

### Overall Justification

One paragraph · 3–5 sentences · at least 150 characters · covers all five dimensions.

**The formula:** name the dimension → give concrete evidence → compare RD vs. AD1 → explain why a client would care.

📍 Always point to a specific place: a timestamp, slide number, cell reference, filename, section, or component ("the wordmark," "the tagline at the bottom").

**✅ Good example**

> "RD's hand-drawn hull and custom-spaced wordmark look designer-made, while AD1's generic wave icon reads as AI (Realism). RD's layout balances the mark and tagline; AD1's tagline crowds the icon (Design). Both are error-free and follow the navy/sand palette (Professionalism, Coherence). RD's tagline matches the mark's line weight across logo.png and logo_dark.png, while AD1's uses an unrelated script (Multi-Asset). A client could use RD on signage today; AD1 would need a redesign."

**❌ Not acceptable**

| Not acceptable | What's wrong |
|---|---|
| "The human one is better overall." | No evidence, no dimension |
| "AD1's looks unprofessional." | Where? Compared to what? |
| "Both are fine, so I gave it a 4." | No reasoning at all |
| "Design is bad, colours are off." | No location, no comparison |

## 6. ✅ Step 4: Reviewer Decision

Answer **Is the Human Deliverable better than the AD1 Deliverable?** Your answer must agree with your SxS scores. Each answer opens a box.

| Your answer | Decision | Box to fill in | What happens next |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yes – Human is better | 🟢 **Go** | None. The Updated Brief screen opens next. | RD is moved forward |
| No – AD1 is better (or it's a tie) | 🔴 **No Go** | "Why is this a No Go?" (30+ characters). Point to the specific issues in RD that drove the decision, or, for a tie, explain where AD1 matched RD. | The response is cleared and the task goes to L-1 |
| Need More Information / Fixes | 🟡 **Not decided yet** | "What information or fixes are needed?" (30+ characters, shown to the contributor) | The task goes back to the same contributor with their work kept |

⚠️ **A tie is not a Yes.** If the deliverables are comparable, answer No.

🤖 **Automatic No Go:** if all five scores are 4 or higher, the tool pre-selects No Go and pre-fills the explanation. Check it and add the specific issues in RD. Don't switch to Need More Information / Fixes here, because the task won't be sent back.

### Brief problems: catch them before you decide

The Updated Brief only appears after a Go, so it can only take small fixes. If the brief has a big problem, don't choose Go. Choose **Need More Information / Fixes** at the decision instead:

- The brief asks for a completely different deliverable than RD or AD1
- The brief is too unclear to compare RD and AD1 fairly
- The brief can't be done as written

If your scores are mixed, decide which deliverable a client would rather receive, and explain in your justification what tipped the balance. For example: *"RD has a spelling error in the headline that makes it undeliverable, so Professionalism outweighs everything else."*

---

## 7. 🛠 Step 5: Updated Brief (Go only)

On a Go, you'll see the Updated Brief, prefilled with the original brief. This is the brief the task moves forward with. Edit it only for small issues that won't require AD1 to be regenerated. If the brief is fine, leave it as it is.

🟢 **Small fixes you can make here**

- Typos or grammar mistakes that change meaning (e.g., the wrong client name)
- A detail that's obvious from the inputs but missing from the brief (e.g., the brand color)
- Unclear wording that could be read two ways
- A minor contradiction ("3 slides" vs. "three to four slides")

ℹ️ **Big brief problems can't be fixed here.** Catch them before you decide and choose Need More Information / Fixes (see Step 4).

## 8. ⭐ Step 6: Quality Measurement

Required on every path except Broken Autoflation Link, including Go and No Go.

| Field | What to enter | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Quality: Overall Task | Rate the task as a whole from 1 to 5. Use the scoring table below. | ✅ Always |
| Feedback | A few specific, actionable sentences for the contributor. They see this directly. | ✅ Always |
| Contributor Quality | High Potential · Limited Potential · Egregious | ⚪ Only if Quality is 1–2 |
| Failure Reason | Missing Domain Expertise · Minor Tasking Error(s) · Major Tasking Error(s) · Low Effort · Suspected AI or Copied Content · Other | ⚪ Only if Quality is 1–2 |
| Other Failure Reason Explanation | Why you chose Other | ⚪ Optional, only if you chose Other |

### How to score Quality: Overall Task

| Score | When to use it | Usually goes with |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Spam only: fake, stolen, unrelated, inappropriate, or clearly low-effort submissions | ⛔ No (spam) |
| 3 | The task is real but can't move forward yet: files are missing, there's nothing to rate, or more information or fixes are needed | ⛔ No (nothing to rate) · 🔧 Needs Fixes · 🟡 Need More Information / Fixes |
| 4 | The project is complete (brief, inputs, and deliverables are all there and okay) but doesn't meet our complexity bar | 🔴 No Go |
| 5 | The project is complete and accepted | 🟢 Go |

⚠️ **Don't use 1–2 for a weak but real submission.** 1–2 is only for spam (fake, stolen, unrelated, inappropriate, or clearly low effort), and it opens the Contributor Quality and Failure Reason questions.

### Writing the feedback

**Formula:** decision → main reason → specific evidence → what to do next time.

| Path | What to cover | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 Go | What made RD stand out against AD1, and any brief edits you made | "Your deliverable clearly beat AI on design and realism. The hand-drawn texture on the hull and the custom wordmark spacing were things AD1 couldn't match. I corrected the restaurant name in the brief ('Harbor & Tie' → 'Harbor & Tide'), to match the input files. Great work." |
| 🔴 No Go | Where AD1 matched or beat RD, and how to make future tasks harder for AI | "AI's version was comparable on four of the five dimensions; your deliverable only pulled ahead on realism. The flat icon style and single-color palette are easy for AI to reproduce. Next time, pick briefs that need more complex composition or multiple coordinated assets." |
| 🟡 Need More Information / Fixes | What in the brief or files is unclear, and what to fix | "The brief asks for 'a set of social posts' but doesn't say how many or for which platform. RD has five Instagram posts and AD1 has three LinkedIn banners, so they can't be compared fairly. Please state the number of posts and the platform." |
| ⛔ No (gate) | Why the task can't be rated | "This task can't be used: the deliverable is built around graphic violent imagery, which we can't accept for training. Please submit original work without unsafe content next time." |
| 🔧 Needs Fixes (gate) | What the contributor must fix or add | "The brief lists input/floor_plan.dwg, but it isn't in the inputs. Please add the floor plan and resubmit. Your work has been kept." |

✅ **Do:** be specific, be kind, and give one clear next step.

❌ **Don't:** paste your justification as-is, be vague ("not good enough"), or make it personal.

## 9. 🚫 Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Scoring a task that can't be rated yet | Answer the gate first. If it's No, Broken Autoflation Link, or Needs Fixes, don't score anything. |
| Choosing No when only the Autoflation Link is broken | Choose Broken Autoflation Link so the task is fixed, not wiped |
| Choosing No when the task has potential | No wipes the task. Choose Needs Fixes so it goes back to the same contributor. |
| Choosing No because the brief is unclear | No is only when the brief or inputs are unusable. Fixable at first sight → Needs Fixes. Only clear during the SxS → Need More Information / Fixes. |
| Flipping the scale (7 when the human is better) | Low = human wins, high = AD1 wins |
| Deciding before scoring all five dimensions | Score all 5 first, then decide |
| Answering Yes on a tie | A tie is No / No Go |
| Choosing Need More Information / Fixes when all scores are 4+ | The tool sets No Go. Keep it and add specifics. |
| Leaving Multi-Asset Consistency unscored for a single image with text | Text and taglines are separate assets, so score it |
| Choosing Go when the brief has a big problem, then rewriting it in the Updated Brief | The Updated Brief is for small fixes only. Catch big brief problems before you decide and choose Need More Information / Fixes. |
| Skipping or rushing the Quality Measurement | It's required on every path except Broken Autoflation Link, and the contributor sees the feedback |
| Judging which one is more "impressive" | Ask which one the client would rather receive |

## 10. ☑️ Before You Submit

- Did I pick the right gate option, and fill in its box?
- Did I score all 5 dimensions separately, in the right direction?
- Is my justification 150+ characters, covering all five dimensions, with specific locations?
- Does my decision match my scores (tie = No Go)?
- Did I fill in the decision box (No Go reason, or the information or fixes needed)?
- On a Go, did I check the Updated Brief?
- Did I complete the Quality Measurement with specific, kind feedback?
